# Chiambucket — Project Overview

Personal portfolio/personal website for Braven Chiam. Dark-themed, design-forward. The current design language is **"Dark Sensory / Signal Archive"** (July 2026 redesign): every page rides a fixed WebGL shader-gradient field, content sits on dark "instrument panels", and one warm ember accent is reserved for live/status moments. Built with Next.js and deployed on Vercel.

**September 2026 clarity + performance pass (branch `tweak/dark-simplify`).** Same design, lighter and plainer. Keep these rules:
- **Background (October 2026, branch `task/background-cards`):** the WebGL shader is gone. `components/Backdrop.tsx` renders one fixed, static `.sa-backdrop` layer (CSS in the "BACKDROP" section at the end of `mainstyle.css`): a soft pool of light at the top of the screen over the warm base (`--sa-base`, cool on `/contact` + `/homelab`), with a faint perfboard dot grid masked to that light. No canvas, no JS, no animation; colours follow `--sa-accent`. The film-grain overlay, cursor spotlight and section glow orbs are retired. Nothing may animate continuously while a page is idle: timers (the tools carousel, the blink easter egg) run only while on screen, and `LazyVideo` pauses loops scrolled off screen.
- **Project cards:** flagship and highlight projects (`data-flagship` / `data-highlight`) keep full cards with their gold edge, two to a row on the homepage. Ordinary projects use compact horizontal cards (`.hp-pf-card.is-compact`). Projects with no write-up (`.is-soon`: Kauli, Series One Light, Copy Board, Web Development, Minecraft) are muted grey and show a non-link "Coming soon" label; their peek summaries say "Write-up coming soon" and keep any live demo link. Nothing links to `/comingsoon` from the project cards or summaries. "Read article" is a real `<a href>`.
- **`backdrop-filter` is opt-in glass only.** A global rule at the end of `mainstyle.css` still forces it off everywhere, so the dark design's old blur rules stay dead (blur over the fixed background cost a re-render per scroll frame). The Apple-style glass surfaces (the sunlit homepage and the nav pills, October 2026) opt back in with `!important`. Keep blur off surfaces nested inside other glass: a nested blur only sees its parent's tint and turns grey.
- **No AI sparkle icons anywhere.** The old uiverse "sparkle" hero button (`.button-hero`, `.dots_border`) and the ✦ glyph after "Read the summary" were removed; "View my work" is a plain `.hp-btn`.
- No looping decorative animations (orbs, aura drift, headline glow, badge pulse, scroll hint, word rotators). **Exception: the sunlit homepage is a "living poster"** (see below), but its loops are transform/opacity only and run only while the hero or closing section is on screen.
- Labels are plain: `--font-ddt` now points at DM Sans, no wide all-caps tracking, no index numbers in the nav, chapter rail or section headers (the big `01/02/03` `.seh-number`/`.peh-number` numerals were removed in October 2026), no `FIG ·` prefix, no bracketed kickers, no `.sa-hud` corner readouts, no Flagship/Highlight tier badges.
- Copy is direct and factual: no mannered prose, no em dashes in visible text, titles `"<Name> | Braven Chiam"`.
- Article loops use `LazyVideo` with posters in `public/images/posters/`.
**Painted scenes on every page (October 2026, branch `task/sunlit-home`).** Every route now sits on a painted scene in the same pastel-poster style, each at its own time of day, and `components/Backdrop.tsx` is gone. `lib/theme.ts` maps routes to themes: `/` sunlit (sage morning), `/photography` golden (golden hour, low sun on the left), `/contact` noon (clear blue midday), `/homelab` teal (misty teal morning), `/credits` and `/comingsoon` mist (lilac haze), `/ai-use` dusk (just after sunset, violet sky, the first faint stars via the preset's `stars` flag; dark glass like the night pages, since `isNight` covers it), and everything else, the articles and the 404 page, `night` (moon, stars, fireflies). The head script and `ClientEffects` (`applyTheme`) write `data-theme` plus `.is-day` or `.is-night` on `<html>`. `components/SceneShell.tsx` (mounted once in `app/layout.tsx`) renders `SunlitScene` keyed by pathname with `theme` and `home`; `PRESETS` in `SunlitScene.tsx` holds each scene's sun or moon position, haze and brushwork tints, rays, cloud colours and layout, and a colour grade for the grass (`gradeField`, canvas blend modes with the field's shape stamped back), and `app/scenes.css` holds each scene's sky gradients (`.sl-sky`, `.sl-wash`, `.sl-sun-bloom` per `data-theme`), which must agree with the preset's sun position. The night preset draws the moon on the sky canvas, two star canvases (`.sl-stars`, the brighter `.sl-twinkle`), and CSS fireflies in the grass instead of birds. Off the homepage the scene renders its own field (no tree, no rising About section) behind all content; it fades on scroll and rises behind the footer at the end like the homepage's. **Day pages** share the light glass in `app/sunlit.css` (now imported by `app/layout.tsx` and scoped to `html.is-day`), which also styles the shared `ct-*` heroes, the contact email card and links, the `cr-*` credits cards and the photography page (its montage hangs as a framed picture on the right); HomeLab's day styles are at the end of `app/homelab/homelab.css` (teal ink, white glass panels, white map nodes). **Night pages** keep the dark-design layout with cool moonlit tokens and a night footer in `app/scenes.css`; article covers are drawn as light on the sky by the `#night-cover` SVG filter in `GlassDefs.tsx` (alpha follows brightness, so black backgrounds vanish), fade out at their edges, and on phones keep to the top of the hero above a night band behind the text; the ELEC-F cover photo (`.art-hero-photo`) is dimmed first so it reads as moonlit. The nav uses `html.is-day .pnav` and `html.is-night .pnav` tokens.

**Sunlit homepage (October 2026, branch `task/sunlit-home`).** The homepage (`/`) uses the light `sunlit` theme (`THEME_MAP` in `lib/theme.ts`). `components/SunlitScene.tsx` paints a sage sky, the sun top right, faint rays, soft clouds, a painted Italian stone pine on the hill at the left, and a grass field, once from a fixed seed and again only on resize. The pine is `public/images/sunlit/stone-pine.webp` (generated with GPT Image 2 and cut out); `drawTree` draws its crown sized to fit between the nav and the name and stretches the bare trunk below it into the grass, measuring the name with `nameBox`. The field is not inside the fixed `.sl-scene`: `SunlitField` (exported from the same file) renders it inside `<main>` right after the About section, so it is drawn in front of About (`#about` is its own `z-index: 0` layer) and behind everything after it; the hero has `z-index: 2` to stay above it. The field is one canvas starting at `FIELD_TOP` (60% of the screen), and is 135lvh tall so it can rise at the end of the page. **Birds:** every 10 to 20 seconds two to four tiny SVG birds (`.sl-bird`) flap and glide across the sky, from one requestAnimationFrame loop that runs only while the hero or closing section is visible, the tab is visible and motion is allowed. (A fox and rabbit that played scenes in the grass were tried and removed.) `app/sunlit.css` (now site-wide for the day pages, scoped to `html.is-day`; see the scenes note above) keeps the dark layout but turns every card, the table, the footer and both pop-ups into Apple-style glass (`--gl-*` tokens: a white tint with a top-left sheen, `backdrop-filter` blur and saturation, a bright rim on the top-left edge, a soft drop shadow), primary buttons and active pills into pine-tinted glass, and text into pine ink (`--sl-*`). In Chromium the glass also refracts the scene at its edges: `components/GlassDefs.tsx` (mounted in `app/layout.tsx`) defines two SVG displacement filters, `#lg-refract` for cards and `#lg-refract-pill` (with a slight RGB prism split) for pills and buttons, and the head script adds `html.lg-refract` only where `navigator.userAgentData` reports Chromium, because url() filters inside `backdrop-filter` only work there. Other browsers get blur and rims. `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` swaps the glass for solid frosted panels. Light text stays only on pine glass and over photos. Scrolling (one rAF-throttled listener in `SunlitScene.tsx`, inline `opacity`/`translate`/`filter`): the About section rises out of the grass, each `.sl-emerge` block soft and faint near the crest and sharpening as it clears it; then the tree and the field sink a little and fade into the haze; near the end both come back and the field rises until its crest is above the footer (measured from `.footer`), so the footer stands in the grass on any screen. With reduced motion the tree and field only fade, the field appears already raised at the end, and nothing blurs; the birds do not appear. The closing CTA's golden glow (`.hp-cta::before`) is off on the sunlit homepage: the section clipped it, and over the raised grass its cut edge showed as a line along the top of the footer. The sunlit footer has no strip: no SYS.ONLINE line, a dark shade over the near grass, cream type and a pine-glass social pill. **Living poster (October 2026):** the hero is `app/PosterHero.tsx` + `app/poster.css` (`.slp-*`): "Braven Chiam" set as large as the screen allows in Figtree 800 (`next/font/google`), made of clear frosted glass, one word per line on portrait screens, over the line "Engineer, designer, all in one". Each word is four stacked copies (`GlassWord`): a shadow with the letter shapes cut out of it, the glass (a backdrop blur cut to the letters with `-webkit-mask-clip: text` where supported, otherwise a milkier tint), a highlight, and a rim. The focus-pull animation uses `animation-fill-mode: backwards`, because a filter left on the word would stop the glass from seeing the sky. No pill, no buttons, no catchphrase, no edge labels, no foreground layer; the nav's "My work" is the only call to action. The name comes into focus once on load (a CSS blur-and-fade, "Chiam" a beat after "Braven") and then holds still: nothing on the type loops (the earlier script lockup, sheen, blurred foreground grass, a plain Instrument Serif name and a bronze-gradient Gloock name were rejected, as was a procedurally drawn tree). `SunlitScene.tsx` paints the sky in layers (pastel washes and brush strokes, a separate rays canvas, one canvas per cloud softened by CSS blur so clouds stay smooth at high pixel density, and the field) and animates them with CSS: clouds drift, rays shimmer, the sun's glow breathes, the tree and the field sway. All of that runs only while `html.sl-alive` is set (an IntersectionObserver on `.slp` and `.hp-cta`), so the glass cards further down are not re-blurred every frame. The About section ("What I do"; layout in `app/about.css`, `.ab-*`) is pulled up under the hero (`margin-top: calc(82lvh - 100svh)`) so its heading sits just under the top of the grass and starts rising on the first scroll. It is four rows: the intro (copy, facts, Sukuna), "From the chip to the cloud" (one glass panel grouping Firmware, Hardware, Software and Infrastructure as a chain joined by a gold trace, left to right on desktop and top to bottom on phones), "Designed for people" (why design matters, then 3D and CAD and Interfaces), then Wireless, Video and Photos. Cells inside a group are translucent with no blur of their own. Each skill tile or layer opens an `InfoModal` with where it was used. The photography tabs and gallery share one glass panel (`.ab-photos`); the closed preview fades out with a mask instead of a coloured gradient. The projects gallery ("Other projects") no longer repeats Project June and BeadReader, which are in the spotlights; `openProject` takes a project id. The separate Capabilities section under Projects, the design-tools carousel and the PowerPoint tile were removed. `public/images/lora-case-cut.webp` is the LoRA case render with its white background keyed out.
The older notes below predate these passes; where they mention a WebGL shader, frosted blur, HUD readouts, flagship tier badges, word rotators or uniform project cards, these notes win.

## Stack

- **Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript** — `app/` directory, server components by default
- **Vercel Analytics + Speed Insights** — loaded in `app/layout.tsx`
- **framer-motion** — installed (`framer-motion`); available for React animations when CSS isn't enough. Prefer CSS for simple transitions; reach for it for orchestrated/gesture/layout animations.
- **Background** — the painted scene of each page (`components/SceneShell.tsx` + `SunlitScene.tsx`, see the scenes note above). The inline head script sets `html.sensory-active`, `data-theme` and `.is-day`/`.is-night` before paint. (The WebGL `SensoryAtmosphere`/`SensoryShell` and the static `Backdrop.tsx` were removed.)
- **Lychee** — self-hosted photo gallery embedded via a remote script (homepage + photography). The remote script *and* its cross-origin stylesheet are injected client-side after paint (a `useEffect` appends a `<link data-lychee-css>`), so the homelab server never render-blocks first paint.
- **No Tailwind, no CSS-in-JS.** One global stylesheet: `public/mainstyle.css` (~3760 lines; the dead legacy pre-Next rules were purged)
- jQuery is **gone** — the old jQuery `.load()` includes were replaced by React components. The legacy `links.js`/`nav.html`/`footer.html`/`projects.js` files and the pre-Next root `*.html` pages have been deleted.

## Shared components (`components/`)

- **`ArticleRecommendations.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — renders a "More to explore" project card grid at the end of every article page. Self-contained: includes all project data, the peek-modal system (same chapter content as homepage), and a "Load more" button (shows 3 cards initially, all on click). Takes an `exclude` prop (the current article's project ID, e.g. `"proj-june"`). Import it at the bottom of any article `main` element.
- **`ProjectPeek.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — the shared "peek" reader (`PeekFeed`) used by BOTH the homepage gallery and `ArticleRecommendations`. Opening a peek shows ONLY the clicked project's reader, then a **"Load more projects"** button (`.hp-rd-feed-more`, reuses `.hp-btn`); pressing it reveals the rest of the gallery (still lazy-mounted per item) plus the END OF FEED marker. `PEEK_ORDER` + `TITLES` + a `PeekModalContent` switch case must include every project id. It is keyed by `startId` at the call sites, so the expand state resets on each open.
- **`ArticleLinks.tsx`** (server component) — a row of key-link pills (`ArtLink[]` with `type: 'github' | 'video' | 'demo' | 'cad' | 'download'`, each with `label` + `url`) surfaced in the article hero, just after `.art-toolrow`. First link renders as the primary (filled) pill; all open in a new tab. Each article page passes its own GitHub / demo / video / CAD links. Styles are `.art-links` / `.art-link` in the ARTICLE section of `mainstyle.css`.
- **`SceneShell.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — mounts the painted scene for the current route (`SunlitScene`, keyed by pathname). Do NOT mount per page; the single mount lives in `app/layout.tsx`. To give a route its own scene, add it to `THEME_MAP`, add a preset to `PRESETS` in `SunlitScene.tsx` and its sky to `app/scenes.css`.
- **Corner HUD readouts (`.sa-hud`)** — bespoke `.sa-hud` blocks (children `.sa-hud-tl/tr/bl/br`, each holding short letterspaced mono `<span>` lines) now sit only in the hero of **homelab/404/comingsoon**. The global `.sa-hud` CSS positions them at the page corners, hides them ≤760px, and boots the lines in with a staggered reveal. Home, photography, contact, credits and every article deliberately do NOT carry corner HUD text (removed at the owner's request), so do not reintroduce it there. Keep any HUD lines short, facts only, no em dashes.
- **`Nav.tsx`** (`'use client'`) + **`components/nav.css`** — the nav as separate small floating pieces (October 2026): the logo on its own (it gets a dark chip once the page scrolls; `ClientEffects` toggles `.pnav.is-scrolled`), a segmented pill of pages (Home, Photography, HomeLab, Contact), and "My work" in a pill of its own at the right (`.pnav-work`), which joins the end of the list when the nav merges (below). The current one sits in a dark inner pill, the mark (`.pnav-mark`): one 3000px-wide element in the page pill, cut to the current item with `clip-path: inset(...)`, so it can also reach My work once merged. Its two edges are animated `@property` lengths (`--mark-l`, `--mark-r`), the leading edge first, so it stretches to the next item and catches up. On the homepage an IntersectionObserver on `#portfolio-items-holder` (a line across the middle of the screen) moves the mark from Home to My work while the projects are there, and back (`aria-current="location"` on My work). Where the mark cannot reach My work (phones, where the page pill is folded away, or the separate pieces under reduced motion) My work gets its own dark background instead. On the homepage Home and My work scroll instead of navigating (Home to the top, My work to the projects); elsewhere My work links to `/#portfolio-items-holder`. Phones (≤760px) swap the page pill for a round menu button that opens a small dropdown (Escape, a tap outside or a route change closes it). Every piece is glass (blur, a bright rim, and in Chromium the `#lg-refract-pill` refraction from `GlassDefs.tsx`); link text carries a soft halo so it stays readable over whatever scrolls under it. **Metaball merge:** in the first 260px of scrolling the pieces slide together into one bar (centred on desktop, at the right on phones), and where two come close a neck of glass reaches across and thickens until they fuse (`components/navBlob.ts` builds the union as one SVG path). While `.pnav.is-blob` is set the pieces drop their own glass: `.pnav-blob-glass` is one frosted layer clipped to the path, and an SVG filter on the same path draws the edge, rim and shadow (`--nv-fill`, `--nv-blob-*` tokens per theme). Scrolling back up pulls them apart. Reduced motion or reduced transparency keeps the separate pieces. The phone dropdown follows the menu button into the merged bar but is clamped to stay on screen. The focus ring uses `--nv-focus`, a plain colour, because `--nv-active` can be a gradient. Colours are `--nv-*` tokens: clear glass with pine ink on the day pages (`html.is-day`), cool moonlit glass on the night pages (`html.is-night`). The old command-bar nav CSS (`.nav-closed`, `#navitem`, `.hamburger`) and the `--sa-nav-a/b` tokens were removed.
- **`Footer.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — shared footer (logo, socials, credits link).
- **`LazyVideo.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — drop-in replacement for a decorative autoplaying `<video>` loop that defers its own weight. Props: `webm`, `mp4`, `poster`, optional `className` / `rootMargin`. The poster paints immediately; the `<source>`s are only injected once the element scrolls near the viewport (IntersectionObserver), and on **Save-Data** connections or **`prefers-reduced-motion`** the clip is never fetched at all (poster stands in). Used by the homepage About-bento media tiles (DaVinci, PowerPoint) and the photography hero montage. Renders a plain `<video>`, so existing CSS targeting those tiles still applies.
- **`ClientEffects.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — global effects: cursor spotlight, nav scroll glow, and the `[data-reveal]` scroll-reveal IntersectionObserver (re-runs on every route change via `usePathname`, so new pages animate in).
- **`ArticleScrollSpy.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — highlights the active chapter in article rails. It observes `<section>` elements and toggles `.article-chapter-selected` on `.article-chapter-wrapper a[href="#id"]`. Article rails therefore carry BOTH classes: `class="art-chapters article-chapter-wrapper"`.
- **`ArticleLightbox.tsx`** (`'use client'`, mounted once in `app/layout.tsx`) — global click-to-zoom for article images. A delegated listener opens any `<img>` inside `.art-body` in a full-screen viewer (click image to toggle a 2.4x loupe that follows the cursor; Esc/scrim/× to close). A floating magnifier cue tracks hovered images as the affordance. CSS (`.art-lb*`, `.art-zoom-cue`, the `cursor:zoom-in` hover-lift) lives in the ARTICLE section of `mainstyle.css`. Opt a subtree out with `data-no-zoom` (used by interactive widgets and tiny icon grids); linked images (`<a><img></a>`) are skipped automatically. Diagrams with transparent backgrounds get a light plate via class `art-diagram` (the lightbox carries it through with `.art-lb-img.lit`).
- **`InfoModal.tsx`** (`'use client'`) — a small, theme-aware detail pop-up (`InfoItem`: eyebrow, title, blurb, optional add / `media` (a wide image) / points / chips / icon, plus either a single `link` or a `links[]` button row, first primary then ghost, that auto-opens http(s) targets in a new tab). CSS-only enter/exit, portaled to `body`, ESC/scrim/× to close. Accents follow the page's `data-theme` via `--hp-*` tokens (blue on home, violet on photography). Used by the homepage **capability cards**, the homepage **About-bento tiles** (intro/engineering/microcontrollers/PowerPoint), and the photography **"My Tools" cards** (each card is `role="button"` with a hover-rotating `+` affordance). Styles are `.im-*` in `mainstyle.css`.
- **Article interactive widgets** (per-route `'use client'` components, not shared) — each flagship article embeds a bespoke framer-motion widget dramatizing its core mechanic: `app/elecf/SafetySequence.tsx` (door/timer stepper), `app/project-june/CommandLink.tsx` (5G signal-path + telemetry HUD), `app/brolocator/LinkDemo.tsx` (LoRa-text vs ESP-NOW-voice two-handset demo), `app/csdp/NodeMap.tsx` (BeagleBone hub-and-spoke node map with an alert simulation), `app/pandus/DispenseDemo.tsx` (dispense-cycle machine with cup-fill), `app/lumen/LumenConsole.tsx` (voice phrase → trigger/Whisper/DeepSeek/MQTT pipeline → parsed JSON → a live "room" panel of devices reacting), `app/beadreader/ReadingTogether.tsx` (live presence "Online now" panel) + `app/beadreader/SpicyGate.tsx` (per-reader content-gate: same passage as Full / No access / Cal mode). Convention when adding interactivity to an article: a scoped `<style>` block with a unique class prefix, `data-no-zoom` on the root, `--hp-*` theme-token accents (plus semantic colours where meaningful), transform/opacity-only animation with a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback, auto-play that yields to manual control **and pauses when scrolled out of view** (gate the auto-advance interval on an `IntersectionObserver` `inView` state), and a single-column collapse at 390px. **Auto-cycling widgets must not reflow the page**: any region whose text changes per step (a paragraph, a wrapping chip row) needs a fixed reserve — the simplest is a hidden "sizer" with the longest content stacked in the same CSS-grid cell (`grid-area: 1/1`, `visibility:hidden`), so the block is always as tall as the worst case at any width (see `SafetySequence` desc + sensor sizers; `LumenConsole` uses fixed-height reserves). Insert inside an existing `<section>` (don't add a chapter to the scroll-spy rail).

## Design system (all in `public/mainstyle.css`)

The file is one large stylesheet, organised by comment sections. Four families matter:

- **DARK SENSORY / SIGNAL ARCHIVE** — the LAST section of the file and the one that defines the current site-wide look. Everything is keyed on `html.sensory-active` (set before paint in `app/layout.tsx`). Tokens: `--sa-accent` (an `R,G,B` triplet following `data-theme`; always use `rgba(var(--sa-accent),x)`, never hardcode accent rgba), `--sa-ember` (`255,106,61`, the ONLY warm accent, reserved for live/status/error moments like status dots, 404, the NEW chip), `--font-ddt` (terminal mono face), `--sa-panel-hi`/`--sa-panel-lo` (frosted panel gradient stops), `--sa-btn` (button-hero fill), `--sa-hairline`. **All of these surface tokens are warm-charcoal by default** (they sit over the warm paper field); the `html.sensory-active[data-theme="indigo"], [data-theme="datacenter"]` block overrides them back to cool navy for `/contact` + `/homelab`. `.button-hero`, the frosted card family, and the `.im-panel`/`.hp-modal-panel` modals all read from these tokens, so surfaces follow the route theme (never hardcode a navy surface). It strips `.hp-band` fills (seams become signal hairlines), frosts every card family into instrument panels, turns `.hp-btn` into ghost terminal buttons, reskins the footer into an instrument panel, restyles `ct-*` heroes, fully reskins the `art-*` article template (bracketed back link, indexed chapter rail, `FIG ·` captions, hero covers mask-dissolving into the field), and provides `.sa-hud`/`.sa-hud-tl|tr|bl|br` corner-HUD classes plus `.sa-live`/`.sa-live-dot`. Panels use `backdrop-filter` over the live canvas, so keep new blurred surfaces to a reasonable count per view.

- **Homepage** — `HOMEPAGE REDESIGN v3 / v3.1 / v3.2 / v3.3` near the end. Tokens live in `:root` as `--hp-*` (`--hp-glass`, `--hp-line`, `--hp-blue`, `--hp-indigo`, `--hp-sky`, `--hp-ink`, …). Reusable: `.hp-band`, `.hp-section`, `.hp-eyebrow`, `.hp-btn` / `.hp-btn-ghost`, `.hp-md-tag.personal|.school|.highlight`, `.hp-pf-*` (project cards/grid), `.hp-cap-*`, `.hp-spot-*`, `.seh-*` / `.peh-*` (editorial section headers), `.icon-stack`.
- **Page heroes** — `.ct-wrap` + `.ct-aura` + `.ct-kicker` + `.ct-title` (with `<em>` gradient accent) + `.ct-sub` (contact/portfolio/photography/comingsoon/404 heroes). `.cr-*` for the credits cards. **Aura gotcha:** keep `<div class="ct-aura">` (or `hp-hero-aura`/`art-hero-aura`) as the first child and let the higher-specificity `> .aura` rule re-assert `position:absolute`, otherwise the `> *` `z-index:2` rule drops it into flow.
- **Articles** — `ARTICLE REDESIGN` section at the very end. Namespace `.art-*`: `.art-hero` (feature header: `.art-hero-bg` img + `.art-hero-scrim` + `.art-hero-aura` + `.art-hero-inner`), `.art-back`, `.art-title`, `.art-lead`, `.art-toolrow`; `.art-body` (2-col grid) with sticky `.art-rail` + `.art-chapters`; `.art-section` blocks (use `data-reveal`); `.art-fig`+`<figcaption>`, `.art-grid` (2-up image gallery), `.art-video` / `.art-embed` / `.art-embed.art-pdf`, `.art-repo` (GitHub callout), `.art-team*` (csdp team rows), `.art-next` (closing CTA). Images are height-capped (`max-height:76vh`, `width:auto`) so tall portrait shots don't blow up the page.

**When building/extending a page, REUSE these classes.** New bespoke CSS for a single page should be a scoped `<style>` block in that page's component with a unique prefix, not a global edit (avoids touching the shared file and prevents collisions).

## Per-page accent theming

Pages are recoloured by a single `data-theme` attribute on `<html>` so each route reads distinctly while staying cohesive. All hues are sampled from the **Frame 6 gradient** (navy → lavender → mauve → violet), saved in `temporary screenshots/Frame 6.png`.

- **`lib/theme.ts`** — `THEME_MAP` (route → scene theme), `themeForPath()`, `applyTheme()`. Superseded in October 2026 by the painted scenes (see the scenes note at the top): the default is now `night` (articles, 404), and the day routes map to `sunlit`, `golden`, `noon`, `teal` and `mist`. The older palettes (`paper`, `indigo`, `datacenter`, `blue`/`violet`/`mauve`/`steel`) still exist as `html[data-theme]` blocks in `mainstyle.css` but no route uses them. Green stays reserved for presence, ember for status.
- **No hardcoded accent colours.** Accent glows/borders/links use `rgba(var(--sa-accent), x)` (per-theme) or the `--hp-*` / `--em-grad` / `--title-grad` tokens, never a literal blue. The July 2026 warm-default pass converted the remaining hardcoded blues (`94,121,219` indigo, `127,168,255` / `140,170,255` sky, `0,107,179`/`10,126,199`/`10,95,176` blue, plus `#5e79db`/`#7fa8ff` colour uses) to those tokens so they follow the route theme.
- **Desktop scale.** The layout is rem/clamp based; `html` root font-size steps down on large screens (`93.75%` ≥1100px, `90.6%` ≥1600px) so the site doesn't read zoomed-in at 1080p. Mobile keeps the full 16px base.
- **Set in two places:** an inline `<script>` in `app/layout.tsx` `<head>` sets it before paint (no flash); `components/ClientEffects.tsx` updates it on client-side navigation (`themeForPath(pathname)`).
- **How it recolours:** `:root` in `mainstyle.css` defines `--aura-1/2/3` (hero/page auras), `--em-grad` + `--title-grad` (gradient accent words), `--orb-edge` (section-number stroke), and overrides `--hp-blue/-deep`, `--hp-indigo`, `--hp-sky`. Each `html[data-theme="…"]` block re-points those tokens. **To theme a new page:** add its route to `THEME_MAP`; to add a new palette, add a `html[data-theme="…"]` block next to the others. Auras/buttons/links/gradient text follow automatically — don't hardcode accent rgba values, use the tokens.
- **The scenes follow the same themes:** each theme name has a preset in `PRESETS` (`components/SunlitScene.tsx`) and a sky block in `app/scenes.css`; night pages also take their `--sa-*`/`--hp-*` tokens from the night block there.
- The homepage hero has a drifting aura plus two free-floating orbs (`.hp-hero::before/::after`); the photography hero has a masked photo-montage backdrop (`index-photos-gif.webm`) + grain. Big section numbers (`.seh-number`/`.peh-number`) are dim with a luminous `-webkit-text-stroke` edge (var `--orb-edge`) for legibility.

## SEO / AI scraping

- **`public/robots.txt`, `public/sitemap.xml`, `public/llms.txt`** — these MUST live in `public/` (Next only serves `public/` at the root; copies in the repo root return 404). Keep `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` in sync with clean routes whenever you add/rename a page.

## Anti-generic guardrails

- **Colors:** custom brand palette only (never default Tailwind indigo/blue).
- **Shadows:** layered, color-tinted, low opacity (never flat `shadow-md`).
- **Typography:** distinct display + body pairing; tight tracking on large headings, line-height ~1.7 on body.
- **Gradients/texture:** layered radial gradients, subtle grain/aura for depth.
- **Animations:** only `transform` and `opacity`; spring-style easing; never `transition-all`.
- **Interactive states:** every clickable element needs hover, focus-visible, and active states.
- **Images:** gradient overlay/scrim for legibility over photos.
- **Depth:** a layering system (base → elevated → floating), not one flat z-plane.
